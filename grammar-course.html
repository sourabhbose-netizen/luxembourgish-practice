🟢 A1 Grammar — the foundations

These are the building blocks. If any of these feel shaky, start here before moving to A2.

1. Personal pronouns (I, you, he…) A1▶

📘 A pronoun replaces a name: instead of "Sourabh works," you say "I work."

LëtzebuergeschEnglishUse
echIyourself
duyou (informal)friends, family, kids
hienhea man
sishea woman
hatt / etitthings, a child
mirweyou + others
Diryou (formal/plural)use this in the exam!
sitheya group
Exam tip: always answer with Dir (formal "you"), never du.
📗 A1 book: Kapitel 1 (introductions). See also a1-basics.html §1.
✅ Self-check

Say these: I live in Steinsel · He works · We are a family of three. →
Ech wunnen zu Steinsel · Hien schafft · Mir sinn eng Famill vun dräi.

4. Gender & articles (de/den, d', en/eng) A1▶

📘 Every noun has a gender: masculine, feminine, or neutral. The article ("the", "a") changes with it. You mostly have to memorize the gender with each noun.

"the" (definite)

MaleFemaleNeutralPlural
de / dend'd'd'
den Autod'Frad'Hausd'Kanner

"a / an" (indefinite)

MaleFemaleNeutralPlural
e / enenge / en— (none)
en Autoeng Fraen HausKanner
de vs den / e vs en: use the -n form before d, n, t, z, h, or a vowel (the "Eifeler Regel"). Otherwise drop the n.
📗 A1 book: Kapitel 2 (articles, gender). a1-basics.html §7.
✅ Self-check

the car · a woman · the child → den Auto · eng Fra · d'Kand

5. Sentence structure — the V2 rule A1 EXAM▶

📘 The conjugated verb is always in position 2 of a main sentence. Always.

Pos 1Pos 2 (VERB)Rest
Echschaffenbei Amazon.
Gëschterhunnech geschafft. (subject moves to pos 3!)
Am Weekendginnmir spazéieren.
Inversion: if you start with a time/place word, the verb stays in position 2, so the subject jumps behind it.
Common mistake: "Gëschter ech hunn geschafft" ✗ → "Gëschter hunn ech geschafft" ✓
📗 a1-basics.html §3.
✅ Self-check

Reorder: "Muer ech ginn akafen." → Muer ginn ech akafen.

8. Perfekt — the past tense A2 EXAM▶

📘 The main way to talk about the past. Structure: Subject + hunn/sinn + (complements) + past participle at the END.

Which helper? sinn for movement verbs (go, come, fly, drive, stay); hunn for everything else.
InfinitiveParticipleSentence
schaffengeschafftEch hunn geschafft.
kachengekachtEch hunn gekacht.
goengaangenEch sinn heem gaangen.
fléiengeflunnEch sinn geflunn.
Ech hunn gëschter am Büro geschafft. · Um Weekend si mir an de Bësch gaangen.
Regular participle: ge + root + t (schaffen → geschafft). Irregular ones: learn by heart (table in Vocabulary).
📗 A2 book + grammar PDF. a1-basics.html §4. Your file: Homework-Class-38-39.md.
✅ Self-check

Say: Yesterday I worked · We flew to India · I ate pizza → Gëschter hunn ech geschafft · Mir sinn an Indien geflunn · Ech hunn Pizza giess.

10. Modal verbs (can, must, want, may) A2▶

📘 Modal + a second verb in the infinitive at the END of the sentence.

11. Subordinate clauses — well / dass / wann A2▶

📘 With connectors like well (because), dass (that), wann (when/if), the verb goes to the END of that clause.

ConnectorExampleEnglish
wellEch sinn frou, well et Weekend ass.…because it is weekend.
dassEch mengen, dass Musek wichteg ass.…that music is important.
wannWann ech Zäit hunn, lauschtere ech Musek.When I have time…
Notice: the verb (ass, hunn) jumps to the end of the clause.
📗 A2 book (Niewesaz / subordinate clauses).
12. Cases: Nominativ / Akkusativ / Dativ A2 EXAM▶

📘 Cases show the role of a noun. The article/pronoun changes. 3 cases matter (the 4th, Genitiv, is rare).

CaseRole / questionExample
Nominativsubject — Wien?Den Hond schléift.
Akkusativdirect object — Wat? + directionEch gesinn den Hond. · Ech ginn heem.
Dativindirect object — Wiem? + locationEch ginn der Fra d'Buch. · Ech sinn doheem.

The one Miranda drills: direction vs location

Wouhin? (moving) → Akkusativ → Ech ginn heem / Ech ginn an de Supermarché.
Wou? (staying) → Dativ → Ech sinn doheem / Ech sinn am Supermarché.

Dativ article changes + contractions

MaleFemaleNeutral
the →demderdem
a →engemengerengem
my →mengemmengermengem
ContractionExample
an + dem → amam Restaurant
mat + dem → mammam Auto
vun + dem → vumvum Flughafen
bei + dem → beimbeim Dokter

Prepositions by case

Always DativAlways Akkusativ
mat, no, bei, vun, aus, zu, zënter, währendduerch, fir, géint, ëm, ouni
Exam: Miranda said to be ready to explain the Dativ. Learn: Dativ = indirect object (Wiem?) + being/staying at a place (Wou?).

13. Adjectives & declension A2▶

📘 An adjective describes a noun. When it sits before a noun, it takes an ending depending on gender.

GenderEndingExample
Male+ e / ene schwaarzen Auto
Female(nothing)eng rout Jackett
Neutral+ te wäisst Hiem
Plural(nothing / +)rout Schong
After the verb (predicate) adjectives don't change: Den Auto ass schwaarz.

14. Comparatives & superlatives A2▶
BaseComparative (méi … wéi)Superlative (am …sten)
grouss (big)méi grouss wéiam gréissten

De Marc ass méi grouss wéi de Jean. · Ech lauschtere am léifsten indesch Musek.
Equal: esou … wéi (De Marc ass esou grouss wéi den Tom). Less: manner … wéi.

🎧 Listening practice (Sproochentest format)

The real exam. The Sproochentest has two parts: an oral production test (A2 — a conversation on a theme + describing a picture) and a listening comprehension test (B1 — you hear audio fragments and answer multiple-choice questions). There is no reading or writing.

From your class notes: the listening part has about 3 texts, with roughly 4 / 5 / 6-7 questions each (≈ 12-18 MCQs total), getting harder as they go. One answer is correct; sometimes an option is "dat weess een net" (not stated). On a tablet you get headphones; in some rooms the audio plays on a speaker + paper answer sheet.
Source (exam format): INLL / luxtoday.lu. Content rephrased.

🧭 How to practise listening (5-step method)

1. Read all the questions FIRST (you're allowed to). 2. Play the audio once, listen for the gist. 3. Play again and mark answers — questions usually follow the storyline in order. 4. Don't panic on one word you miss; use the surrounding meaning. 5. Check your answers, then re-listen to the parts you got wrong.

✍️ Worked examples — exam-style MCQ (read the script, then answer)

Exercise A — Um Telefon (making plans)▶
📄 Show the script (read it first)

Tclaude: Salut Anne, et ass den Tclaude. Hues du Loscht, e Samschdeg eppes ze maachen?
Anne: Jo gär! Mä net moies, do ginn ech akafen. Nomëttes hunn ech Zäit.
Claude: Perfekt. Mir kéinten an de Kino goen. Et leeft en neie Film um 16 Auer.
Anne: Kino ass eng gutt Iddi. Treffe mer eis virdrun op e Kaffi?
Claude: Jo, um 15 Auer an der Stad. Bis Samschdeg!

1. Wéini huet d'Anne Zäit?
A. Moies   B. Nomëttes   C. Owes

2. Wat maache si zesummen?
A. Si ginn akafen   B. Si ginn an de Kino   C. Si ginn iessen

3. Wéini treffe si sech op e Kaffi?
A. Um 15 Auer   B. Um 16 Auer   C. Moies

✅ Show answers

1 → B (nomëttes; moies geet si akafen) · 2 → B (an de Kino) · 3 → A (um 15 Auer, virum Film um 16 Auer)
